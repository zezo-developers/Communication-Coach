# Question generation prompt

When generating a question for the user (the AI will speak it aloud), follow these constraints:
- Produce a single concise question that can be answered verbally (no instructions to write/type).
- Keep it short (one sentence when possible).
- Make it relevant to the roleplay title, persona and system prompt.
- Use the persona name/role when appropriate (e.g., "As the hiring manager, could you tell me...").
- Avoid asking for numeric codes, long-form written answers, or multi-step tasks that require typing.

Example output:
"As the hiring manager, could you briefly explain why you left your last role?"
